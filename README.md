# 🎀 Pretty Rack

### Your closet, your creativity, your style.

Pretty Rack is a cute, creative outfit-building website designed to help you rediscover your closet, express your personal style, and make more outfits with the clothes you already own.

---

## ✨ About Pretty Rack

Pretty Rack was created by two high-school sophomores who love fashion and care about the environment.

We believe fashion should be fun, sustainable, and accessible. You don't need to constantly buy new clothes to create amazing outfits!

## 🌷 Features

- 👚 **Outfit Builder** — Mix and match clothing pieces to create outfits.
- 🎀 **Personalized Styling** — Explore different aesthetics and outfit vibes.
- 💖 **Lookbook** — Save and revisit your favorite outfit combinations.
- ✨ **Random Outfit Generator** — Get inspired with unexpected combinations.
- 👜 **Closet Inspiration** — Rediscover pieces you already own.
- ♻️ **Sustainable Fashion** — Encourage creativity and reduce unnecessary shopping.

## 🌸 Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire people to express themselves through fashion while encouraging sustainable closet habits.
